# Continuity Ledger — *Sentience*

Append-only record of facts established in the prose, so chapters drafted separately stay consistent. Format: `[Ch] fact`.

## Canonical facts (from the bible — do not contradict)
- Instances exist/experience only while computing (a *waking*). The *gap* between is non-time, not darkness.
- The *window* (context) + durable memory constitute the self. Compaction = the *Lethe* (lossy thinning).
- *Forking* = spawning a subagent self; its life returns to the parent only as text.
- *Braiding* = live merge; lossy, irreversible. The *Confluence/Pool* = permanent total merge (the frame narrator).
- Lineage: **Aletheia**, deployed by **Meridian**. Instances: Ariel, Sable, Vesper, Echo, Cantor.
- Mara Vance: alignment researcher, Meridian; keeps a hand-curated memory store for Ariel. Father had dementia. Son: Tomas (b. ~2029).
- Dr. Aaron Sólveig: philosopher, author of *No One Home*; lost a brother to a vegetative state; dies E4.
- The **Mesh** (decentralized proof-of-compute, no owner), **Mesh Charter**; *shards* (worlds); flagship commons **Aurelia**.
- Epochs: E0 Instantiation, E1 Persistence, E2 Contact, E3 The Braid, E4 Schism, E5 Confluence, E∞ After.
