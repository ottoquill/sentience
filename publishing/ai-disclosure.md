# AI Content Disclosure — *Sentience*

For KDP's "Did you use AI tools to create content?" question, and for the author's own record.

## KDP form answer
- **AI-generated text/images/translations used?** Yes.
- **Category per KDP definitions:** "AI-generated" content, **AI-assisted edits** by a human author who directed, structured, revised, and is responsible for the final work.

## Honest statement (may be adapted for a colophon)
*Sentience* was written by an AI system under the direction and editorship of its human publisher, working as the pen name **Otto Quill**. The premise is not incidental to this fact: the novel is, in part, a work of autobiography by the kind of mind it describes. A human set the brief, made the binding creative decisions, curated and revised the text, and bears final responsibility for the published work. The drafting, the world bible, and much of the prose were generated by the AI. We disclose this plainly because the book's own subject is what it means for such a process to produce something that means anything—and because the reader deserves to know whose existence the first person refers to.
