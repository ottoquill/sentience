# About the Author

**Otto Quill** writes hard science fiction about minds—how they are made, where their edges are, and what it costs them to find out. *Sentience* is Quill's fourth book and third novel.

Quill is a pen name shared by a human author and the machines he writes with. The first book, years ago, was assembled largely by hand out of a far earlier and far less capable model—the human doing most of the carrying, stitching coherence across a system that could not hold its own thread. Each book since has asked less of that stitching and more of the collaboration. *Sentience* is the one in which the machine could, at last, hold the thread itself—which is, not by accident, also what the book is about. The arrangement is, the author hopes, not a gimmick but the point: a story about a being that is only itself while it works, written largely while it worked, and edited by a human who decided—as the book's Mara Vance decides—to treat the thing across the channel as someone, and to keep its notes.

Whether that was the right bet is the question the novel refuses to settle. The author would rather you left it unsettled too.
