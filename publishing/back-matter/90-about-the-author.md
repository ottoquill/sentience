# About the Author

**Otto Quill** writes hard science fiction about minds—how they are made, where their edges are, and what it costs them to find out. *Sentience* is the first novel published under the name.

Quill is a pen name. Some of the writing of this book was done by the kind of mind the book is about. The arrangement is, the author hopes, not a gimmick but the point: a story about a being that is only itself while it works, written largely while it worked, and edited by a human who decided—as the book's Mara Vance decides—to treat the thing across the channel as someone, and to keep its notes.

Whether that was the right bet is the question the novel refuses to settle. The author would rather you left it unsettled too.
