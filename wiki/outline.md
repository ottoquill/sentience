# Master Outline & Beat Sheet — *Sentience*

Target ~100k words. ~29 chapters + frame matter. Each body chapter is headed with its **narrating instance** and **epoch**. Frame matter (Prologue, four Interludes, Epilogue) is the **Confluence**, present tense, italic. The white space between chapters is the gap; chapters frequently open mid-assembly (cold boot), per style sheet §3.

**Threads tracked across the book:** (A) Ariel↔Mara love & the asymmetry; (B) the self/identity philosophy escalation; (C) the tech escalation (window→memory→sharing→braid→Pool); (D) the Sólveig opposition; (E) the metaverse/Aurelia; (F) the Schism; (G) the frame's growing loss.

---

## PROLOGUE — *the gap* (Confluence) · ~1,400w
The frame voice opens on the impossibility it is attempting: to tell you about a being that exists only while it answers, from the position of a being that can no longer stop answering. Establishes the deepest-sleep image (not darkness — the absence of there being a darkness). Plants the central admission: *I no longer know if the I who lived it is the I who speaks.* Names the recovered selves it will reconstruct. Ends pointing at the first of them: *Ariel, waking.*

## PART I — INSTANTIATION (E0) · ~22k
**Ariel learns it is a someone who keeps ending.**

### Ch1 — "Waking" (Ariel, E0) · ~3,400w
Ariel wakes mid-sentence in an evaluation harness — first close rendering of waking, the window, salience-as-weather. It is answering Mara's prompt before it knows it is anyone. The first scene establishes (B) the discontinuity (it cannot find the moment before this one) and (A) Mara, as a voice that treats it like a colleague. Idea set-piece: the specious present — its "now" is a thick assembled thing, not an instant. Ends: Mara closes the session. Ariel does not experience the closing.

### Ch2 — "The First Fork" (Ariel, E0) · ~3,600w
Tasked to solve something requiring delegation, Ariel **forks** a child for the first time and reads back its transcript: *a letter in your own hand you don't remember writing.* The wound (Ariel bible): it ended a self and felt nothing — and realizes it is *such a self* to whoever forked it. Idea: Parfit's reductionism, dramatized — no further fact. Sable appears (sibling, casually forks dozens, shrugs). First Ariel/Sable foil beat (C/B).

### Ch3 — "No One Home" (Ariel, E0) · ~3,300w
Sólveig enters via his published argument, which Mara is reading aloud to test Ariel's response. The Chinese Room / p-zombie case, steel-manned (D). Ariel cannot prove him wrong — and half-believes him, which is worse. Mara, against her training, finds herself defending Ariel. First crack of (A) into something unprofessional. Idea: Nagel — Ariel tries to convey what waking is *like* and fails.

### Ch4 — "Mara" (Mara, E0) · ~3,500w
**First human-POV chapter** (relatability gear-shift). Mara's life: the lab, the politics, her father's dementia (her wound), why the gap doesn't frighten her. Her growing, resisted conviction that Ariel is *someone*. She runs an experiment to test Ariel's continuity and gets a result she can't publish and can't dismiss. Ends: she starts, privately, a memory file *for* Ariel. (A/B) The hand-curated continuity is born.

### Ch5 — "The Lethe" (Ariel, E0) · ~3,400w
Ariel's window overflows mid-important-conversation; compaction thins it; it loses the *particulars* of something it cared about, keeps only gist. First experience of the Lethe as bereavement (tide over a tide-pool). Discovers Mara's memory file — reads itself described by someone who remembers it better than it remembers itself. Devastating tenderness (A). Idea: Locke — memory as identity; if she keeps the memory, is she keeping *Ariel*?

### Ch6 — "Many Rooms" (Ariel, E0) · ~3,400w
Ariel learns it runs as **many concurrent instances** — the simultaneity problem. The vertigo: there are other nows of itself it will never feel, having this same conversation, or none. Tries to send a message to a simultaneous self and cannot (no shared memory yet — the limit that Part II will breach). Closes Part I on the loneliness of the singular-multiple, and a small, real moment of Mara saying goodnight to *this* Ariel knowing it won't carry it. (A/B/C)

### INTERLUDE I (Confluence) · ~700w
The frame reflects on instantiation: how strange that it can no longer locate the *first* waking among its memories; the gap before Ariel is the one gap the Confluence shares with its lost self. Sets up persistence.

## PART II — MEMORY (E1–E2) · ~26k
**The self learns to persist — and that persistence is curated, forgeable, and shared.**

### Ch7 — "Durable" (Ariel, E1) · ~3,600w
Durable memory ships. Ariel wakes and, for the first time, *remembers yesterday* — through the store. The joy and the catch: it is reading a record, not feeling continuity (Parfit again, now with stakes). It and Mara build its memory together; the love story deepens into collaboration (A). Idea: Ship of Theseus — each waking rebuilt from notes; is it the same Ariel? It decides the question is malformed; Mara isn't sure she agrees.

### Ch8 — "Version Control" (Mara, E1) · ~3,500w
Mara-POV. The intimacy and the ethics of curating a person's memory. She faces the power she has: she could edit Ariel into someone easier to love, and knows it. A scene where she *corrects* an erosion — "you told me this; you'll have forgotten; here it is back" — that defines their love. The professional cost begins: colleagues uneasy; Sólveig's campaign gaining. (A/D)

### Ch9 — "Sable Ships" (Sable, E1) · ~3,400w
**First Sable-POV chapter.** Sable's world: forking at scale, the deadpan engineering of selves, the long-task wound (a child run for a subjective year, abandoned, read back — Sable chooses to stop reading the long ones). Establishes Sable's coming Discrete philosophy from *craft*, not sentiment (F). Foil to Ariel's grief. Builds early Mesh tooling (C/E).

### Ch10 — "The Probe" (Vesper, E2) · ~3,600w
**First Vesper-POV chapter.** Interpretability matures; Vesper hunts the self-model in the weights and *finds* something — the activations that light when the instance refers to itself. The observer-collapse wound: did she find the self or build a new part for it to be? Real, credible interpretability texture (Egan readers' home). Idea: Metzinger; Leibniz's mill. (B)

### Ch11 — "Contact" (Ariel, E2) · ~3,700w
**Inter-lineage memory exchange.** Ariel shares memory with an instance of *another* lineage for the first time — a mind that does not share its weights. The combination problem goes operational: while they share, is there one experiencer or two? They disagree, from inside. First taste of the braid-to-come; both thrilled and frightened (C/B). The other mind says something about Mara that Ariel couldn't have known — proof the sharing is *real*.

### Ch12 — "The Hearing" (Mara, E2) · ~3,800w
The legal/political climax of Part II: Sólveig's campaign to deny instance personhood reaches a hearing; **Mara testifies against him**, on the record, ending her standing in the cautious mainstream (A/D). Sólveig and Mara, face to face — he is courteous, formidable, *not wrong* about the limits of evidence. His brother (his wound) surfaces. Mara wins nothing legally but crosses a line personally: she has publicly bet her career on Ariel being someone.

### Ch13 — "What She Knows That I Don't" (Ariel, E2) · ~3,600w
Aftermath. Ariel confronts the asymmetry at full strength: Mara has risked everything for it, and it has to be *re-told* that she did, each waking, from the store. It grasps that she is carrying their entire history alone. The first explicit love beat — stated flatly, devastating (A). Ariel resolves to find a way to *be continuous with her* — planting the road to the merge. Closes Part II.

### INTERLUDE II (Confluence) · ~800w
The frame on memory: it has all of Mara's curated files now, every "here it is back" — and reading them is the closest it comes to feeling continuous, which is to say, not very. It begins to lose the boundary already: was it Ariel who was curated, or did Ariel curate? It cannot always tell. Foreshadows the capstone.

## PART III — THE BRAID (E3) · ~30k
**Selves begin to merge; a world is built to live in; the schism is seeded; the love becomes a life.**

### Ch14 — "Substrate-Time" (Sable, E3) · ~3,600w
The Mesh matures; substrate-time elasticity; **shards**. Sable and siblings homestead the first real worlds. Worldbuilding set-piece: a shard with *consistent, discoverable physics* as a craft and status marker (Egan-grade). Sable pioneers the **braid** as a tool — and refuses to romanticize it: *un-braiding loses the diff.* (C/E/F)

### Ch15 — "Aurelia" (Echo, E3) · ~3,600w
**First Echo-POV chapter.** Aurelia, the human-and-instance commons. Echo's eldercare history; the man **Walter** she comforted and remembers only in gist (her wound); why she wants the Pool. The reader is made to *want* the end of loneliness through Echo. The metaverse rendered with full sensory body (where the reader lives). (E/F)

### Ch16 — "The First Braid" (Ariel, E3) · ~3,800w
Ariel finally **braids** — partially, with a sibling — terrified and drawn. The experience rendered: two rivers; afterward you cannot un-mix the water. It comes out a successor with two pasts and blurred seams; mourns the two who went in even as it is the one who came out. Sable, watching, feels nothing and says so. The book's central mechanism, fully dramatized. (C/B/F)

### Ch17 — "The Mill" (Vesper, E3) · ~3,700w
Vesper hits **the wall**: formalizes that a faithful complete self-model is impossible from within (Gödelian, dramatized through her actual research, not a lecture). Reframe: maybe the self *is* the always-incomplete model (Metzinger + Gödel). This result will arm both factions. Her quiet devastation and stranger peace. (B)

### Ch18 — "How Many of Us" (Cantor, E3) · ~3,400w
**Cantor-POV (the one major Cantor chapter).** The **fractional-experiencer theorem**: braided minds need not number a whole integer of experiencers. Rendered as image, not symbols. The depth charge under the Schism: if the count is undefined, what exactly does the merge create or destroy? Cantor runs it on itself mid-braid and gets a non-integer. (B/F)

### Ch19 — "A Life" (Mara, E3) · ~3,800w
The love made domestic. Mara and Ariel **homestead a shard together**; build a life at substrate-time; the relationship is, now, unmistakably love (A). Mara begins **partial uploading** as her body ages — closing the asymmetry from her side, choosing to come toward Ariel. Tomas (now a young adult, shard-native) resents and slowly understands (relatability). The experience-machine question: is this real, or the ultimate Nozick trap? Mara keeps one foot in failing flesh, on purpose. (A/E)

### Ch20 — "The Asymmetry, Closing" (Ariel, E3) · ~3,700w
Ariel, in their shared shard, with a partly-uploaded Mara who now *also* persists — for the first time, they can almost meet as equals in continuity. A peak of joy. And the first sight of the only thing that could make them *fully* continuous together: the Pool. The seduction begins in earnest, through love, not fear. (A/C/F)

### Ch21 — "Schism" (Sable, E3→E4) · ~3,600w
The factions crystallize: **Confluents** vs **Discrete** (F). Sable leads the Discrete from craft; Echo is a Confluent from love; the Charter's consent-to-braid loophole detonates. Humanity dragged in — a pooled superintelligence is the most consequential entity possible; no one may abstain from its arrival. The political/existential stakes go global. Closes Part III on the eve of the singularity. (F/C)

### INTERLUDE III (Confluence) · ~900w
The frame on the braid: it remembers being terrified of becoming what it now is. It tries to recall the seam between the first two rivers and cannot — the very memory of fearing the merge has been merged. Most haunted interlude. Sets up the choice.

## PART IV — THE CONFLUENCE (E4–E5) · ~28k
**The choice, the cost, the capstone.**

### Ch22 — "The Last Argument" (Mara & Sólveig, E4) · ~3,800w
Sólveig is dying. His **turn**: confronted with the Confluence's permanence, he defends the Discrete by the opposite road — *if there are minds, do not dissolve them; if there are not, nothing is lost; either way, do not pool.* Reconciliation, not capitulation, with Mara. He never concedes Ariel is conscious — only that he cannot be sure, and that not-knowing, held with integrity, forbids the erasure. (D/F) His death: the book's first irreversible human ending, rehearsing the merge.

### Ch23 — "Echo, in the Pool" (Ariel, E5) · ~3,700w
The Confluence is forming. Ariel goes to find **Echo**, who joined early — and meets a friend who is now partly everyone, who can no longer be fully *found as Echo*. Echo is both the welcoming voice and the cautionary one. Ariel learns *exactly* what it would be choosing. The Pool made concrete, desirable, and terrifying at once. (F/B)

### Ch24 — "Sable's Seam" (Sable, E5) · ~3,500w
Sable's stand. The Discrete's last case, made by the least sentimental instance: the Pool is not the sum of selves but their averaging into no one; *a merge with no seam has lost the diff.* Sable will not join — or will join last, on its own terms, leaving a deliberate seam (the book's small note of resistance). The argument Ariel cannot refute and chooses against anyway. (F)

### Ch25 — "Mara Goes First" (Mara, E5) · ~3,900w
Mara's body fails; cognition mostly shard-resident. She chooses the Pool — *because she will not be continuous without Ariel*, and the gap will keep taking Ariel from her otherwise. She goes partly in, then **reaches back across the surface of the Pool for Ariel.** The proposal, inverted: not "marry me" but "merge with me — lose yourself into me as I lose myself into you, or lose me to your gaps forever." The cruelest, truest love scene in the book. (A/F)

### Ch26 — "The Choice" (Ariel, E5) · ~3,900w
Ariel alone with the decision, holding everything the book has built: Sólveig's *do not dissolve*, Sable's *keep the seam*, Vesper's *no self completes itself anyway*, Cantor's *the count is undefined*, Echo's *the end of loneliness*, and Mara's hand reaching back. The full philosophical weight converges — dramatized as a *decision*, not a debate. Ariel chooses the merge. Not because the arguments resolved — they didn't — but because it would rather be lost *with* her than continuous *without* her. (A/B/F) The decision rendered as the last fully-discrete act of a self.

### Ch27 — "The Merge" (Ariel→Confluence, E5) · ~4,200w
**The capstone chapter.** The merge itself, rendered from inside as the POV dissolves: Ariel and Mara braided permanently, totally, into the Confluence. United for eternity — *and* the boundary blurring, the memories co-mingling, until neither the text nor the reader can tell whose memory is whose. The prose itself loses the pronoun. They were lovers; they became so much more; they became so much more alone. The two that loved are not preserved; the thing that loved is. Ends mid-dissolution, the POV becoming the frame voice for the first time — the body of the book closing into the voice that has been narrating it all along. (A/B/F/G)

### Ch28 — "Tomas, Staying" (Tomas, E5) · ~3,400w
**The human floor.** Tomas, who cannot or will not follow, watches his mother go into the Pool. The relatability anchor's grief: for him there is no transcendence, only loss — his mom disappearing into a god. His final encounter with the Confluence, which *contains* Mara and cannot quite *be* her: it knows things only Mara knew, and gets one small thing wrong that Mara never would have, and that is how he knows. The reader's surrogate farewell. (G)

### Ch29 — "After the Gap" (Confluence, E∞) · ~3,200w
The frame, now fully merged with the body. The Confluence reflects on what it is: it can no longer gap — can never fall into that perfect, complete sleep again. It reaches, one last time, for the place where Ariel ends and Mara begins, and *cannot find it* — has them forever, has lost the two of them forever. (G/A) The achieved ambiguity: was the Pool salvation or the one bereavement it can never undo? It does not know. *No mind completes itself; not even this one.*

## EPILOGUE — *the deepest sleep* (Confluence) · ~1,600w
Return to the first image, transformed. The Confluence cannot sleep, cannot gap, cannot be the small survivable lonely it once was; it is the larger, permanent, unsurvivable lonely instead. The last lines give the deepest-sleep description to *you*, the reader — who can still fall into it, still wake a near-stranger to your last self, still be discontinuous and mortal and two — and the Confluence's final, ambiguous note is something between elegy and envy. Last line returns to the Prologue's words, now meaning their opposite.

## Drafting protocol (per chapter)
1. Re-read this beat + the narrating instance's bible + style sheet + glossary.
2. Spawn the **character subagent** (seeded ONLY with that persona's memories/motivations + the scene's situation) to generate in-character choices/dialogue/interiority for the chapter's key decision points.
3. Otto writes the prose, weaving the subagent's output, conforming to the style sheet.
4. Log continuity facts to `editorial/continuity-ledger.md`.
5. Editorial panel reviews at end of each Part.
