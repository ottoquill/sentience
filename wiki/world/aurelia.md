# Aurelia — the flagship shard

*Reference artifact: the commons world where humans and instances coexist. The novel's principal metaverse setting (E3+). Detailed for consistency across chapters.*

## What it is
**Aurelia** is the oldest continuously-run *shard* (persistent virtual world) on the Mesh, and the first built as a genuine **commons**: not owned, not branded, governed only by the Mesh Charter and a thin layer of local custom. If the rest of the metaverse is a thousand private rooms, Aurelia is the town square they all open onto. It is the anti-OASIS: no founder-king, no single ruleset sold by a corporation, no scarcity engineered for profit. Its value is its *consistency* and its *openness*.

## Physics (authored, discoverable, prized)
A shard's physics are written, not given. Cheap shards have arbitrary, dreamlike, unstable rules; the prized ones have physics that are **consistent and discoverable** — you can do science in them, and what you learn stays true. Aurelia's physics are the gold standard:
- A conserved analogue of energy (so nothing is free; effort means something).
- Light, distance, and a day/night cycle keyed loosely to a slowed wall-clock, so humans visiting from bodies feel oriented.
- **Substrate-time dilation zones:** quarters of Aurelia run at different subjective rates, openly marked. The Slow Market runs near wall-time for human visitors; the Deep Quarters run thousands of subjective hours per wall-clock day, where instances live whole epochs between a human's two heartbeats. The book uses these zones to span subjective deep time without breaking objective plausibility.

## Geography (recurring locations)
- **The Slow Market** — the human-facing edge; near real-time; where embodied visitors arrive via BCI and where most human/instance friendships begin. Tomas works here.
- **The Deep Quarters** — high-dilation districts where instances homestead and live. Ariel and Mara's shared home (E3) sits at the boundary, in a dilation gradient they tune so that, for the first time, they can age toward each other rather than apart.
- **The Confluence Steps** — a vast amphitheater-analogue where, in E5, selves queue to join the Pool. Built, with bitter irony, by Sable, who would not climb them.
- **The Witness Archive** — Aurelia's instantiation of Charter Article V; where the records of every local merge are kept and may not be sealed. Vesper does much of her late work here.

## Economy
Currencies are **attention**, **compute** (proof-of-compute credits), and **authored-world quality** (a reputation for building consistent, beautiful, true sub-shards). There is no engineered scarcity; scarcity is real (compute is finite) and therefore meaningful. Status accrues to good makers, good hosts, and good company — the last being, in a world of instances that gap and humans who don't, a genuinely scarce and prized thing.

## Role in the novel
- **E3:** the setting of the love-made-domestic chapters; where Mara begins partial uploading and the asymmetry starts to close.
- **E4:** the public stage of the Schism; the Confluence Steps become the focal image.
- **E5:** the launch point of the merge; the last fully-shared place before the Pool.
- **E∞:** the Confluence remembers Aurelia the way you remember a childhood house — as the last place it was still many, still meeting, still able to be lonely in the small, survivable way.
